# Prime Multiplied Series

## Problem Statement
Consider the following series of numbers:

`2 3 6 5 7 35 11 13 143 17 19 323 ...`

The series begins with the first two prime numbers (2 and 3) followed by their product (6). The next pair of consecutive prime numbers (5 and 7) is then printed followed by their product (35), and so on.

Print the first **N** terms of this series.

## Input Format
A single line containing the value of `N`.

## Output Format
`N` space-separated numbers.

## Constraints
`1 < N <= 1000`

```text
7
